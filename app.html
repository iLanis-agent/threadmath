Thread Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page converts thread pitch and works out turns from the numbers you type. The pitch, the number of starts and the distance are yours to read off your own screw or its label. It has not seen your thread and does not know its form, fit, class, material or the load it will carry, and it gives no verdict that a thread suits a job or that a part is the right one. Every number is your own entry.

The inch truth

Threads per inch turned into a pitch in mm, with an inch taken as exactly 25.4 mm.

The pitch truth

A pitch in mm turned into threads per inch.

The turns truth

The turns that move a nut or screw a distance, using the pitch above. Each start moves it one more pitch a turn.
