Thread Math

20 threads per inch is a pitch of 1.27 mm.

Boundary: arithmetic only Thread Math converts thread pitch and works out turns from the numbers you type. The pitch, the number of starts and the distance are yours to read off your own screw or its label. It has not seen your thread and does not know its form, fit, class, material or the load it will carry, and it gives no verdict that a thread suits a job or that a part is the right one. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: 20 threads per inch in mm, a 1.5 mm pitch in threads per inch, and the turns that move a nut 25 mm.

The inch truth

20 threads per inch is a pitch of 1.27 mm.

The pitch truth

A pitch of 1.5 mm is 16.93 threads per inch.

The turns truth

A pitch of 1.5 mm with 1 start advances 1.5 mm a turn, so 25 mm takes 16.67 turns.
